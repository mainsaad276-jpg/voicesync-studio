Refund policy

Last updated: October 2026. This is a starting template — have it reviewed for your business before relying on it.

Eligibility

You can request a refund within 7 days of purchase if you have used less than 10% of the characters bought.

Failed generations

Characters for failed generations are returned to your balance automatically — no request needed.

How to request

Message us on WhatsApp () or email with your invoice number. Approved refunds are sent back to the original EasyPaisa, JazzCash or bank account within 5 business days.

Cancellation

Plans do not auto-renew, so there is nothing to cancel — simply don’t renew.
