Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. This is a starting template — have it reviewed for your business before relying on it.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, hashed password.
  • Content you provide: scripts, voice samples you upload, generated audio.
  • Payment records: plan, amount, payment method and transaction ID you submit. We never receive your EasyPaisa, JazzCash or bank login.
  • Basic technical data: IP address for abuse prevention and rate limiting.

How we use it

To provide the service, process generations, verify payments, prevent abuse, and contact you about your account. We do not sell your data.

Processors

Speech generation and voice cloning are performed by our AI infrastructure partners, who process your text and samples only to produce your audio.

Voice samples

Voice clones are private to your account unless you choose otherwise. You can delete a voice at any time.

Retention & deletion

You can delete individual generations, voices, or your entire account from Settings. Payment records may be kept as required by law.
