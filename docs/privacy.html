VoiceSync Studio — Privacy Policy

Website and Android app · Effective 9 October 2026

← Back to VoiceSync Studio

In short

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics or tracking.
  • We do not collect or store your text, voice or files. Our only server is a small voice relay (Cloudflare Worker) that passes your text to Microsoft Azure to make Studio voices and keeps nothing.
  • To make a voice, the text you type is sent to the voice service you choose (listed below). Phone/computer voices stay on your device.

These services have their own privacy policies. Do not type passwords or private personal details into the script box.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you tap Record My Voice or Voice to Text (a short explanation is shown first, in your language). Recordings are saved on your device. A recording is uploaded only if you tick the voice-cloning consent checkbox and then generate with the Chatterbox voice, or create a clone with Create My Clone, as shown in the table.

What is stored on your device

  • Settings (language, chosen voice, presets, Easy Mode) in the browser/app storage.
  • Files you export. In the Android app they are saved to Documents/VoiceSync.

Clear them any time by deleting the files or clearing the app's / site's storage.

Website hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/mainsaad276-jpg/voicesync-studio/issues.
